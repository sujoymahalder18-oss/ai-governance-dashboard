# AI Governance Dashboard

A free, single-file template for tracking and reporting AI governance. Open `index.html` in any modern browser. Nothing is uploaded and no account is needed.

## What it does
- Records an AI inventory with a risk level, an approval decision and a decision date
- Holds a starter library of 55 controls that merge ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF 1.0 requirements, without listing the same requirement twice
- Shows an executive summary with a red, amber or green status, a roadmap, actions, a risk grid and a change history
- Lets you rename lists, pages, columns and rules, and add your own frameworks and fields
- Imports and exports CSV (paste from Excel) and JSON

## Important
- It is a tracking and reporting layer. It does not replace policy, risk assessment, evidence or audit work.
- It opens with invented sample data. Use "Clear sample data" in the banner to start with your own.
- Data is saved in your own browser only. Use Settings, then Export all, to keep a backup.
- Control statements are summaries in our own words. The mapping to ISO/IEC 42001 and the NIST AI RMF is indicative. Check the published standards before relying on it.
- Not legal, audit or regulatory advice.
